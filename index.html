About

Bolla is Murwillumbah’s neighbourhood pizza club. Naturally fermented dough, a wood-fired oven, local and Italian ingredients where we can get them, and a record always on.

No bookings, no fuss. Walk in Thursday to Sunday from five, grab a table and stay for another glass, or call ahead and take a box home.
